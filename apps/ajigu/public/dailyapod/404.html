FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is DailyApod free?

Yes—downloading and browsing the daily picture is free. DailyApod Pro (a one-time purchase or subscription) unlocks extras like unlimited caption translations. Check the App Store listing for current pricing.

Where do the pictures come from?

NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD), published daily since 1995. Each image links back to its original source and credits its authors.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or later, and it runs on Mac and Apple Vision Pro too. Home screen widgets require iOS 16+.

What about my privacy?

DailyApod collects no personal data. Favourites sync via your own iCloud account, and translation happens on device.
