from the `@shadcn` registry, using the Radix new-york style. Marketing pages
compose these components with neutral tokens and product-specific content.
Import aliases and accessible slider labels are adapted to this workspace.
The original MIT licence is retained in `packages/ui/licenses/shadcn-ui.txt`.
