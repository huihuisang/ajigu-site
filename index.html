What we believe

Small choices, made carefully.

Every ajigu app shares the same three promises.

1

Privacy first

Analysis happens on your device. No accounts, no ads, no tracking—your data stays yours.

2

Light by design

Each app does one thing well. No subscriptions to manage, no dashboards to maintain, no clutter.

3

Details that delight

Software can be playful. We sweat the small interactions, because the small interactions are the product.
